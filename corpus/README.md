# Historical research corpus

`raw/` contains research dossiers and adversarial reviews produced while choosing the architecture and building the persona. The position/style dossiers preserve public source links used by the prompt in `personas/ck-gender.md`.

These are **historical research outputs**, including estimates, competing proposals, and claims challenged by other dossiers. They are not an authoritative list of current product capabilities, a model training dataset, or a certification that political/statistical claims are true. Current implementation details are in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; setup is in `docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md`.

For the public export, developer-specific absolute paths have been normalized and upstream `file://` references have been converted to links to the pinned source revision where possible. Private Claude session archives and runtime recordings are not distributed. External publishers retain rights in their source materials and quotations; see `docs/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
